What is the Champions League?

The UEFA Champions League is the top club competition in Europe. It started in 1955 as the European Cup.

Since 2024/25 there are no groups. All 36 clubs play in one league table and each club plays 8 matches.

How the season works

01

League phase

September to January. Every club plays 8 matches against 8 different teams.

02

Knockouts

February to May. Play-offs, Round of 16, quarter-finals and semi-finals.

03

Final

5 June 2027 at the Estadio Metropolitano in Madrid.
